## 2) BGM：Suno 生成提示词（可直接粘贴）

### `menu.ogg`

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
1950s diner lounge jazz with a light swing groove, upbeat and welcoming.
Upright bass walking, brushed drums, warm piano chords, vibraphone lead, occasional muted trumpet stabs.
Playful “restaurant business” vibe, confident but cozy, not cheesy.
Tempo ~110 BPM, major key, vintage tape warmth, subtle room reverb.
Seamless loop, no hard ending, ~2:00 length, clean mix, low fatigue.
```

### `game_calm.ogg`

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
Mid-century lounge / bossa nova hybrid for strategic thinking.
Soft bossa rhythm (brushes + shaker), mellow electric guitar comping, upright bass, piano, light marimba.
Calm, focused, slightly mischievous, background-friendly.
Tempo ~95 BPM, minimal lead melody, loopable groove.
Seamless loop, no big finale, ~2:00 length, warm mix.
```

### `game_intense.ogg`

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
Upbeat swing / big-band-lite for dinner rush and competitive tension.
Driving ride cymbal, walking bass, energetic piano riffs, punchy brass hits (short stabs), subtle sax riffs.
Tempo ~145 BPM, bright but not harsh. Keep motifs short and loopable (avoid long solos).
Seamless loop, ~2:00 length, crisp mix with a touch of vintage color.
```

### `game_over.ogg`

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics.
Short end theme (0:35–0:45). Retro jazz outro, bittersweet but resolved.
Slow tempo ~80 BPM. Piano + upright bass + soft brushed drums, gentle string pad or vibraphone.
Clear ending chord with a tasteful fade-out. No loop needed.
```
